**The Hypixel Guardians** maintain two Discord bots for one Hypixel SkyBlock guild community. Pick a product
from the switcher in the sidebar (or the links below) — each has its own navigation and docs.

## Products

| Product | What it does |
|---------|--------------|
| [TriBridge](/tribridge) | Bridges Discord and Hypixel **guild chat** — relay, tags, officer chat, and in-game guild commands |
| [THG Community](/community) | The community **Discord bot** — profiles, account linking, feature requests, and shared staff roles |

Both bots serve the **same Discord server** and share one **PostgreSQL database** for account links and
bot-admin roles. TriBridge reads what the community bot writes; see
[Shared database](/community/shared-database) for the contract.
