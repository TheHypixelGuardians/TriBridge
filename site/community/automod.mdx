---
title: "Automod"
description: "Invite links blocked and timeouts for repeat offenders, on top of Discord's own AutoMod."
---

Discord's own AutoMod does the blocking. The bot builds on top of it: it adds a rule that blocks invite links,
and it times out members whose messages AutoMod keeps blocking. Both are switched on in `/setup` > **AutoMod**.

## What members notice

- An invite link to another Discord server is blocked before anyone sees it, with a short note saying why.
- If AutoMod blocks your messages several times in a short while (by default 3 times in 10 minutes), you get a
  short timeout (by default 10 minutes) and a DM with the reason.

## The server's own rules

The AutoMod page lists every AutoMod rule in the server: what it matches, what it does and what is exempt.
Keyword lists show only as counts.

**The bot never changes these rules.** Staff edit them in **Server Settings > AutoMod**, so changes made by
hand there are never overwritten.

## Invite links

**Block invite links** creates one extra AutoMod rule, *Block Invite Links (THG Community bot)*. It matches
`discord.gg`, `discord.com/invite`, `discordapp.com/invite` and `dsc.gg` links.
- Bot admins are exempt. Add more exempt roles or channels to the rule in Server Settings.
- When a mod log is set, Discord posts an alert there for each blocked invite.
- Because it is a native rule, it works even while the bot is offline.
- **Stop blocking invites** deletes the rule.

## Timeouts for repeat offenders

The bot counts the messages AutoMod blocks for each member, from every rule. When a member reaches the limit
within the time window, the bot times them out:

| Setting | Choices | Default |
|---------|---------|---------|
| Blocked messages | 2, 3, 4, 5 | 3 |
| Within | 5, 10, 30 or 60 minutes | 10 minutes |
| Timeout length | 5 min, 10 min, 1 hour, 6 hours, 1 day | 10 minutes |

Each timeout is a normal [moderation](/community/moderation) case, with the bot as moderator, a DM to the
member and a line in the mod log. Bot admins, server administrators and anyone above the bot's role are
never timed out.

The bot needs **Manage Server** and **Moderate Members**. The AutoMod intent it uses is not privileged, so
nothing has to be switched on in the Developer Portal.
