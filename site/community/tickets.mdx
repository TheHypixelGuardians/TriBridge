---
title: "Tickets"
description: "Open a private channel with staff from the ticket panel."
---

Members open a private channel with staff by pressing a button on the ticket panel. Each ticket has a number
and a channel called `ticket-0042`. The member fills in a short form first, and a transcript goes to the log
when the ticket closes.

## Opening a ticket

Press the button for what your ticket is about:

| Type | You are asked |
|------|---------------|
| **Support** | What you need help with |
| **Guild application** | Your Minecraft name, SkyBlock level and why you want to join |
| **Report a member** | Who you are reporting and what happened |

A guild application checks your Minecraft name with Mojang, and a name that doesn't exist is refused. If you
have linked your account with `/link`, your name is filled in for you.

You can have **one open ticket of each type**. Pressing the same button again points you to the one you have.

Only you, the ticket staff and the bot can see the channel.

## Inside a ticket

- **Claim** (staff) shows who is handling the ticket. Press it again to unclaim.
- **Close** asks for an optional reason. You can close your own ticket, and staff can close any ticket.
- `/ticket add user` and `/ticket remove user` (staff) bring another member in or take them out.
- `/ticket close [reason]` works like the Close button.

When a ticket closes, you get a DM with the reason, the transcript goes to the staff log, and the channel is
deleted a few seconds later. Staff can reopen a closed ticket from the log for 24 hours. It gets a new
channel with the same number.

## Setting it up (staff)

On the **Tickets** page of `/setup`, pick:

- the **panel channel**
- the **category** where ticket channels are made
- the **staff role**
- the **log channel**

Then press **Post the panel**. Posting again replaces the old panel. Members can't open tickets until a
category is set.

The bot needs these permissions:

- **Manage Channels** and **Manage Roles** server-wide. Manage Roles is Discord's name for editing a
  channel's permission overwrites, which `/ticket add` and `/ticket remove` do. The bot never gives anybody
  a role.
- View Channel, Send Messages, Embed Links, Attach Files and Read Message History in the category.
- Attach Files in the log channel.

Transcripts need the **Message Content** intent switched on for the bot in the Discord Developer Portal.
Without it, the transcript lists the messages but not their text.
