---
title: "Moderation"
description: "Warn, time out, kick and ban through the bot, with numbered cases and a mod log."
---

Moderators warn, time out, kick and ban members through the bot. Each action becomes a numbered **case**, is
posted to the mod log and is sent to the member by DM.

## Commands

| Command | Needs | What it does |
|---------|-------|--------------|
| `/warn <user> <reason>` | Moderate Members | Records a warning and DMs it to the member |
| `/timeout <user> <duration> [reason]` | Moderate Members | Discord's timeout: `30m`, `12h`, `7d`, at most 28 days |
| `/untimeout <user> [reason]` | Moderate Members | Takes a timeout off early |
| `/kick <user> [reason]` | Kick Members | Kicks the member, after the DM |
| `/ban <user> [reason] [delete_messages]` | Ban Members | Bans a member, or a user who isn't in the server (paste their id). Can delete their last hour, day or week of messages |
| `/unban <user> [reason]` | Ban Members | Lifts a ban |
| `/purge <count> [user]` | Manage Messages | Deletes up to 100 recent messages in the channel, optionally only one user's |
| `/cases <user>` | Moderate Members | The member's latest 25 cases |
| `/case view <id>` | Moderate Members | One case |
| `/case reason <id> <reason>` | Moderate Members | Changes the reason: your own cases, or any case as an admin |
| `/case delete <id>` | Administrator or bot admin | Removes a case from the history. The mod log keeps a line |

Members without the permission don't see the command. The permission is checked again when the command runs,
because a server administrator can change who sees a command in Discord's settings.

`/purge` skips pinned messages and messages older than 14 days, which Discord doesn't allow bots to delete in
bulk.

## Who can be acted on

The bot refuses when:

- the target is you, the bot or the server owner
- the target's top role is equal to or above yours, unless you are the owner. Without this check anybody with
  Kick Members could use the bot to kick someone above them, which Discord itself would refuse.
- the target is a **bot admin** and you are not a server administrator, so a moderator can't remove the
  people who run both bots
- the bot's own role is not above the target's, or it lacks the permission

## What the member is told

The member gets a DM with what happened, in which server, the reason and the duration. It is sent **before**
a kick or ban, since it can't reach them after, and after a warn or timeout. If their DMs are closed, the
action still happens and the case records that the DM failed.

## Cases and the mod log

A case is written only **after** Discord accepted the action, so the history never shows a ban that didn't
happen. Every case is posted to the mod log channel, which falls back to the general log channel. Purges,
reason changes and deleted cases are logged there too.

Actions taken by hand in Discord (right-click, Ban) don't go through the bot, so they are not cases.

## Setting it up

Run `/setup`, pick the **Moderation** page and choose the mod log channel.

The bot's role needs **Moderate Members**, **Kick Members** and **Ban Members** for the matching commands, and
**Manage Messages** plus **Read Message History** in a channel for `/purge`. Its role must sit above the roles
of the members it acts on. No extra intent is needed.
