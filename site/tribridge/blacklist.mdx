---
title: "Blacklist"
description: "Bar a member from both bots' commands and from relaying into guild chat."
---
`/blacklist` bars a member from **both bots** — TriBridge and the THG community bot. It is bot-admin only and
every reply is private.

```
/blacklist add user:@Someone reason:Spamming the bridge
/blacklist remove user:@Someone
/blacklist list
```

## What it does

A blacklisted member:

- has every command, button, menu and autocomplete refused, on both bots;
- no longer reaches Minecraft from the bridge channel or an [officer channel](/tribridge/officer-chat). Their
  message stays in Discord with a ⛔ reaction, so they can see it was not sent rather than it disappearing.

What they type **in-game** is not affected, because the blacklist is about Discord accounts. Use `/mute` for
guild chat.

## Rules

- **Bots, yourself and other bot admins cannot be blacklisted.** Take the
  [admin role](/tribridge/admin-roles) away first. Otherwise one admin could lock another out of both bots.
- **A change applies here at once**, and on the community bot within about 15 seconds.
- **It fails open.** If the database cannot be read, nobody counts as blacklisted. Failing closed would refuse
  every command and stop the whole bridge for everyone during an outage, to keep out a handful of members. The
  admin check still fails closed, so nothing privileged opens up.
- The reason is for the other admins and is never shown to the member.
- Every add and remove is recorded in the [audit channel](/tribridge/auditing).

## Where it is stored

In the database shared with the community bot, in its `Blacklist` table, per Discord server. TriBridge writes
it — the only table in the community bot's schema it does — and the community bot reads it. The table is
created by the community bot's migrations, so **update the community bot first**: until its migration has run,
`/blacklist add` reports a database error and nobody is blacklisted.

The ⛔ needs **Add Reactions** in the channel. Without it the message is still held back, just without the
marker. See [Permissions](/tribridge/permissions).
